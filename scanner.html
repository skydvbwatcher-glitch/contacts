Scanner sites

Broadcastify ↗Largest directory of live police, fire and EMS feeds. Browse by country, state/province, county. OpenMHz ↗Free trunked-system calls with a built-in player (US). RadioReference ↗Live audio feeds plus frequency databases for every area. Scanner Radio ↗Curated popular feeds and big-city scanners. LiveATC ↗Air traffic control (aviation, not police/fire).
