Live ADS-B data from aircraft within 25 nm of each airport. Aircraft photos are representative images of the same aircraft type, not necessarily the exact aircraft being tracked; photo credits and licenses link to Wikimedia Commons. Airline and route (from → to) come from a public route database and may occasionally be wrong or missing, especially for small planes and charters. Arriving/departing uses the route when known, otherwise it is estimated from altitude, climb rate and heading. Arrival estimates are based on current distance and speed, not an airline schedule. Gate assignments, boarding times and schedule-based arrival times are not available from ADS-B; those need a separate airline or airport schedule feed.
