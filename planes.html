The worldwide map is provided live by ADS-B Exchange. Searching for an airport switches the nearby map and aircraft list to ADS-B data within 25 nautical miles. Airport names and locations come from the daily-updated OurAirports open-data directory. Aircraft photos are representative images of the same aircraft type, not necessarily the exact aircraft being tracked; photo credits and licenses link to Wikimedia Commons. Airline and route (from → to) come from a public route database and may occasionally be wrong or missing, especially for small planes and charters. Arriving/departing uses the route when known, otherwise it is estimated from altitude, climb rate and heading. Arrival estimates are based on current distance and speed, not an airline schedule. Gate assignments, boarding times and schedule-based arrival times are not available from ADS-B; those need a separate airline or airport schedule feed. For airport boards open YYZ board ↗ · YHM board ↗. Aircraft that don't broadcast ADS-B (some military) are not shown.
